Meeting

This first pass visualises the existing, persistent local boardroom conversation as one meeting. Formal start/end and separate meeting records are not implemented yet.

Send immediately asks one available intelligence to respond. A leading address such as “Hey Mistral” gives that participant the first turn; otherwise the most recent active supplier responds. Spontaneous interruptions are not implemented yet. Public utterances are stored in Snug's record and the selected previous exchanges are provided when a supplier is called.

Moving a participant outside currently affects which model can be called in this browser, not server-enforced access to previous meeting history. That limitation must be resolved before guest access or confidential table discussions.
